04 · Review panel

Before the grilling

Decisions for review

  1. First adapter: decided: UXP plugin (Photoshop, then Premiere). The browser adapter waits for legal sign-off.
  2. Companion alone? Yes: runs fully offline with local briefs. No: needs cloud for brief intake and brand guides.
  3. Adobe terms check before any build? Yes / no. BLOCKING
  4. Local vs synced: step log, outputs and brand assets stay local; sync only briefs, guides and analytics? Yes / adjust.
  5. Higgsfield in MVP? Keep behind its gate, or drop until after MVP?

Open risks

  1. Adobe terms: automating logged-in apps may breach usage terms.
  2. Credential isolation: the browser profile and UXP token must never reach cloud or agents' logs.
  3. UI brittleness: web Adobe app selectors change without notice.
  4. No Playwright installed yet: browser adapter is unproven on this machine.

Cut to fit the budget

  • Map: web Adobe apps merged into the browser adapter node; Firefly + Higgsfield share one dotted node; analytics sync is a sublabel, not an arrow (pattern caps 8 components, 10 paths).
  • Sequence: Companion UI merged into the Studio Lead lifeline (cap 5); Lead→Editor dispatch and "write output" folded into notes and the alt guard (cap 12 messages); revise loop shown as a label, not a second fragment.
  • States: "Draft brief" became the start dot with SUBMIT (cap 9 nodes); "abort from any" and "pause from any running" are notes, not arrows.
